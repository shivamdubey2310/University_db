# **Mission Statement**

The mission of the Northview University Academic Database is to provide a centralized, accurate, and efficient system for managing student, faculty, course, and enrollment information. This database will ensure seamless tracking of academic records, scheduling, and grading while maintaining data integrity and enforcing institutional constraints such as room availability and conflict-free student schedules. By supporting comprehensive reporting and secure access, the system will empower administrators, faculty, and students to make informed decisions and enhance the overall academic experience at Northview University.

----

# **Mission Objectives**

1. **Centralize Academic Data** – Maintain a single, consistent source of truth for student, faculty, course, and department information.
2. **Streamline Student Management** – Store and manage detailed student profiles, including majors, minors, and enrollment history.
3. **Support Faculty & Department Operations** – Track faculty assignments, department affiliations, and course offerings efficiently.
4. **Enhance Course & Scheduling Management** – Manage course details, classroom assignments, and term-specific offerings while preventing room and schedule conflicts.
5. **Facilitate Accurate Enrollment & Grading** – Record student enrollments, track academic progress, and store grades securely.
6. **Enforce Academic Constraints** – Ensure no scheduling conflicts for rooms or overlapping classes for students.
7. **Enable Reporting & Analytics** – Provide data for academic performance reports, scheduling optimization, and resource allocation.
8. **Ensure Data Security & Integrity** – Protect sensitive information through controlled access, validation rules, and backup strategies.
9. **Support Institutional Growth** – Allow scalability for future expansion of programs, courses, and enrollment volumes.
